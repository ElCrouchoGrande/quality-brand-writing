# Brand layer onboarding

Run this when the user asks to set up their brand voice, naming rules, or company style, or accepts your offer to do it. Go one step at a time. Ask short questions and wait for answers. Offer to skip any step.

## 1. Check what exists

Look for `references/brand-voice.md` and `references/style-and-naming.md`. If either exists, show the user what is in it and ask whether to update it or leave it alone.

## 2. Voice

Ask:

1. What is the company, team, or person this writing is for?
2. Do you have written voice or tone guidelines? If yes, ask them to paste the text or give a file path. If no, go to question 3.
3. Describe the voice in three or four words. What should it never sound like?
4. Who reads this writing, and what do they care about?
5. Does the voice change by channel (press, sales, social, support)? How?
6. Are there example pieces that sound right? Ask for one or two.

Draft `references/brand-voice.md` from `templates/brand-voice.template.md`. Use their own words and keep it under 500 words. Show the draft and revise it with them before saving.

## 3. Style and naming

Ask:

1. US, UK, or another English variant?
2. Which product, service, or feature names must be written exactly one way? Ask for the exact spelling, capitalization, and punctuation.
3. Any trademark or legal marks (TM, ®) and when they are required?
4. Terms with fixed spelling (for example "email" vs "e-mail") or words to avoid?
5. Anything else a reviewer always fixes?

Draft `references/style-and-naming.md` from `templates/style-and-naming.template.md`. Show it and revise it before saving.

## 4. Banned phrases (optional)

Ask whether they want to add words or phrases to the banned list in `SKILL.md`. Add only what they name.

## 5. Privacy check

If the user pastes internal or confidential brand material, tell them: `references/brand-voice.md` and `references/style-and-naming.md` are listed in `.gitignore`, so they stay out of a public repo. If they plan to share the skill, they should leave those two files out.

## 6. Test

Ask for a short real piece of copy. Rewrite it with the brand layer applied and ask whether it sounds right. Fix the reference files based on what they say.
