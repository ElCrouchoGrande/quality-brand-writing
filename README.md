# quality-brand-writing

A Claude skill that keeps prose plain, direct, and human. It strips marketing hype and obvious AI phrasing from emails, posts, web copy, and other writing.

## What it does

- Prefers simple words, short sentences, and conversational grammar
- Bans hype phrases ("game-changing", "unleash", "leverage") and AI filler ("in today's fast-paced world")
- Keeps your facts, names, and intent intact when rewriting

## Make it yours

Ask Claude to "set up my brand voice". It walks you through a few questions and writes two private files: `references/brand-voice.md` and `references/style-and-naming.md`. Both are git-ignored, so your company's material stays out of a public repo.

## Install

Copy this folder to `~/.claude/skills/quality-brand-writing/` (Claude Code), or upload `SKILL.md` as a skill in Claude.
